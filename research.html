GLiDE, a decision model

Sahibzada Allahyar, Fastino Labs

Training GLiDE Weights Decision Index arXiv:2610.02762 Formal verification

I am the creator of Fastino GLiDE. GLiDE v1.2 is a frontier decision model from Fastino Labs. It ranks first on the Decision Index v0.4.1, with a full score of 71.5, 1.7 points ahead of the next model. Weights are on Hugging Face.

I have trained multiple state-of-the-art frontier models. These models beat models from top frontier labs, including OpenAI’s Decision API, Baseten’s Mammoth-1, Perplexity’s Decider, and Cloudflare’s Clef.

The training account is How to Train a SOTA Frontier Decision Model. The first release is Introducing GLiDE: The First Thinking Decision Model, with Mary Newhauser. Both are listed under artefacts.

Publications

Sam Wang, Julia White, Sahibzada Allahyar, Dhruv Atreja, Urchade Zaratiana, and Kelton Zhang, Dynamic LLM Routers are Often Misguided, arXiv:2610.02762 (2026). Under review at NAACL. Four other manuscripts are under peer review at tier-1 journals.

Formal verification

Formal-Verification-RSI has about 2,900 GitHub stars. It is a Lean 4 and Dafny proof checker that catches fake AI proofs (reward hacking), together with verified recursive self-improvement for coding agents. The work is for mathematics, physics, and software engineering.
